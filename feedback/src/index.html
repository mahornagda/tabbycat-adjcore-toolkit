For judges

Everything that was written about your judging, in one piece.

Teams and co-panellists wrote about you after their rounds. All of it has been read and consolidated into one honest summary of how you judged.

The same private link you used to submit ballots and feedback. Pasting the whole URL is fine — only the key at the end is used, and it never leaves your browser.

What this is, and what it deliberately is not

  • No scores. No feedback score, no average, no rank, no count of who wrote in. None of it is here and none of it is coming.
  • Anonymous. Nobody is named, and nothing says whether a point came from a team or from someone on your panel.
  • Round-blind. Nothing tells you which debate a comment came from — no round, no motion, no argument, no team.
  • Yours alone. Your page is reachable only from your own private key. Nobody has a list.

Written by AI from the written comments only, then read by the CAP. It is a synthesis, not a transcript, and it paraphrases throughout — deliberately, because a distinctive phrase would identify whoever wrote it.
